My Story

I've always loved art and creating things, but I never thought of it as a career. Like a lot of people, I grew up hearing the "starving artist" stereotype, so I chose a more traditional path and started college in Computer Science. After realizing it wasn't the right fit for me, I switched to Computational Data Science. While I enjoyed the problem-solving side of it, I still felt like something was missing.

While looking into different career paths, I came across UX design. It was the first time I realized I could combine creativity, technology, and problem-solving into one career. It also changed the way I looked at coding. I realized I don't dislike programming. I enjoy using code to bring ideas to life, whether that's building websites, creating interactive experiences, or turning a design into something people can actually use.

That realization inspired me to pursue a second major in Art and Visual Technology with a focus in Graphic Design and User Experience. Today, I enjoy designing digital experiences that are intuitive, visually engaging, and enjoyable to use, and I'm always looking for ways to keep learning and improving.
